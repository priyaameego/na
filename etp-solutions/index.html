What Is an Effluent Treatment Plant?

An Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) is a system designed to treat industrial wastewater before discharge or reuse. It removes contaminants through physical, chemical, and biological treatment processes.

  • Primary, secondary & tertiary treatment
  • Reduction of BOD, COD, TDS & TSS
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Water reuse & sustainability
