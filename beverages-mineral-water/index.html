What Is a Mineral Water & Beverage Plant?

A mineral water and beverage plant processes raw water through advanced purification systems and packages it into bottles, jars, or pouches under hygienic and controlled conditions.

  • Raw water treatment & purification
  • RO, UV, Ozonation systems
  • Automated bottling & packaging
  • Quality testing & compliance
