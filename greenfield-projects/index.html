What Is a Greenfield Project?

A Greenfield Project involves setting up a manufacturing facility from the ground up, covering planning, engineering, installation, commissioning, and operational stabilization.

  • Plant layout & capacity planning
  • Equipment & utility installation
  • Safety and compliance systems
  • Trial production & handover
